# City expansion

The playable map roster contains exactly Metro City, Queens, and Spider-Punk City. Hong Kong was removed. The home screen keeps the suit selector on the left and places the map selector and Start Game control on the right. Maps can also be changed through the Spidey Tracker. Day/night is independent of Clear blue, Golden hour, Rose dawn, Overcast, Snowfall, and Snowstorm skies; Spider-Punk City is restricted to Snowfall and Snowstorm. Escape releases the captured pointer.

Full-detail nearby imports share geometry and materials. Instanced source-derived horizon geometry fills visible blocks out to the camera's 2,800 m range, fading into fog by 2,600 m. Snowstorm deliberately reduces visibility. Repeating triangle collision remains independent of render distance. This is an infinitely repeating imported district, not uniquely generated neighborhoods.

The horizon assets are generated by scripts/build-city-horizons.mjs. Bounded spatial simplification reduces detail while preserving original texture coordinates. The closest horizon tier reuses the source building textures and materials; silhouettes behind it remove texture maps. Distant floors bridge tile edges. Sky clouds use continuous three-dimensional direction noise rather than a longitude coordinate with a wrapping edge. Snow coats upward-facing city surfaces and uses camera-local falling particles.
